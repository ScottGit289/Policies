SprintLab Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 July 2026

SprintLab is built on a simple principle: your training data belongs to you and stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing.

SprintLab has no user accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We operate no general backend, and we never receive a copy of the data you enter or the health metrics you sync. The only time the app uses the network at all is if you choose to connect a wearable (see below). Your health data is fetched directly between your device and the wearable maker. The single exception is the Oura sign-in step, which is relayed through a small SprintLab-operated service so that Oura's confidential app secret never has to be shipped inside the app — this is explained in “Connecting a wearable” below.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter (athlete profile, personal bests, training sessions, notes, injury notes, sleep ratings, settings) — plus any health metrics you sync from a connected wearable — is stored locally on your iPhone, inside the app's private storage area, protected by iOS's built-in data protection. Wearable sign-in tokens are held in the iOS Keychain and are never written to a backup export.

Connecting a wearable (optional, off by default)

SprintLab can link to a wearable (such as Oura, Garmin or Hume Band) to blend recovery data — sleep, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate — into your training readiness. This is entirely optional and switched off until you enable it and sign in.

When you connect one:

  • Sign-in uses the vendor's official OAuth page. SprintLab never sees your wearable account password.
  • Your device fetches the health metrics you approved directly from the vendor's servers. That health data flows straight to your phone and is stored there — it never passes through, or is stored on, any SprintLab service.
  • Oura only: completing Oura sign-in requires Oura's confidential app secret, which cannot safely live inside a phone app. So the Oura sign-in hand-off — the step that turns your approval into an access token — is relayed through a small SprintLab-operated service (running on Cloudflare at auth.sprintlab.uk and apitoken.sprintlab.uk) that holds that secret. This service processes only the sign-in exchange to complete the connection; it is not sent, and does not store, your Oura health data (that still comes straight to your phone). Garmin and Hume Band use the direct flow with no such relay.
  • You choose the scope of data at the vendor's consent screen, and you can disconnect at any time, which deletes the saved sign-in and all synced health data from your device.
  • Your use of the wearable and its own app remains governed by that vendor's privacy policy.

If you use iCloud device backups, your SprintLab data is included in your device backup the same way other app data is — handled entirely by Apple under your iCloud settings, never seen by us.

Data that leaves your device

Data leaves your device only when you explicitly export it using the “Export backup” button, which hands a copy of your data to the iOS share sheet. You choose the destination (Files, AirDrop, email, etc.), and you are responsible for wherever you send it.

Data deletion

You can delete all SprintLab data at any time via Settings → Delete all data inside the app, or by deleting the app itself. Your entries and synced health metrics live only on your phone, so there is no copy anywhere else to delete. (The Oura sign-in relay described above stores none of your data — only your device holds it.)

Children

SprintLab is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no data from anyone, we collect no data from children.

Future features

If a future version ever offers an optional online feature (for example, AI-assisted coaching), it will be off by default, require your explicit consent, show you exactly what would be shared before anything is sent, and this policy will be updated first.

Changes
