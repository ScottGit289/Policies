SprintLab Support

A local-first training lab for sprinters — readiness, analytics and personal bests, all on your device.

Need help? Email support@sdtuk.com and we'll get back to you.

Do I need an account?

No. SprintLab has no sign-up and no login. You can use every feature straight away.

Where is my data stored?

Everything you enter — profile, personal bests, sessions, daily metrics and any synced health data — is stored on your device only. There is no cloud copy.

Do I need a wearable?

No. The app works fully with manual entry. If you have an Oura Ring you can optionally connect it to auto-fill recovery metrics — go to Settings → Wearables, turn on wearable sync, and tap Connect. You can disconnect any time, which removes that device's data.

How do I back up or move my data to a new phone?

Open Settings → Your data → Export backup to save a backup file wherever you like (Files, email, AirDrop). On another device, use Import backup and choose that file to restore everything.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings → Delete all data. Because everything lives on your device, deleting it there removes it completely.

How is my readiness score worked out?

It blends recovery signals (sleep, HRV, resting heart rate and more) with your own check-in and training load into a 0–100 score and a Sprint / Gym / Recovery / Rest recommendation. Every value is editable, and each is labelled by where it came from.
